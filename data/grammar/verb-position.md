# Main Clauses

In a normal German main clause, the conjugated verb is in position 2.

Examples:

- Ich lerne Deutsch.
- Heute lerne ich Deutsch.

The first position can be the subject, a time expression, or another phrase. The verb still
stays in position 2.

# Questions

In yes/no questions, the conjugated verb comes first.

Examples:

- Lernst du Deutsch?
- Kommst du heute?
